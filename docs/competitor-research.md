# Competitor UX research — November 2026

What we looked at: product websites and help centres, YouTube walkthroughs (frames from Houzz Pro's proposal tutorial; Indian quotation-tool videos), and user reviews on Capterra, Software Advice and comparison blogs. Reddit threads could not be reached from our research tools, so Reddit is not represented here.

## Players reviewed

| Product | Market | What it is | Ease-of-use signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houzz Pro | US/UK, global | All-in-one: leads, estimates/proposals, invoices, 3D, takeoffs | 4.3★ (1,089 Capterra reviews). Praised for "everything in one place"; criticised as clunky, "too many steps", the new version "too technical". |
| Programa | AU/UK/US | Studio ops: schedules, specs, procurement, client dashboard | 4.8★ ease of use. Praised as clean and intuitive. Criticised for weak insight into what a project earns; users want to hide unused fields. |

| DesignFiles | US | Boards + proposals for small studios | 4.5 ease; some say "basic functions require constant workarounds". |
| Mydoma Studio | CA | Client portal + proposals | 3.9 ease; "hard to maneuver, lots of tags". |

## What users consistently complain about
1. **Too many menus and steps.** The most common complaint, across Houzz Pro, Studio Designer and Mydoma.
2. **Rigid tables.** People want to hide fields they don't use.
3. **Starting from a blank page.** Fast tools start from templates, past quotes, pasted Excel rows or a short prompt.
4. **Not knowing what a project earns** (Programa's main criticism).
5. **Clients can't easily review or approve** without logging in.

## Patterns worth adopting
- One working surface for the quote, not separate screens per step.
- Start with a draft, not an empty table.
- Measurements drive quantities (room size → floor, wall, ceiling, perimeter). This is how Indian studios work in Excel take-off sheets.
- Paste straight from Excel.
- Clear "what's left to do" guidance.
- Copy a previous project.
- Share on WhatsApp.
- Finish tiers / packages (laminate · acrylic · PU, or essential · premium · luxury).
